    Common Inquiries

    What AV equipment is included?

    Our standard corporate package includes high-lumen laser projectors, motorized screens, integrated surround sound, and high-speed enterprise Wi-Fi. Additional tech like translation booths and hybrid-stream kits are available upon request.

    Is there sufficient parking for 500+ attendees?

    Yes, we offer over 400 complimentary on-site parking spaces with professional attendant services available for high-capacity events to ensure smooth entry and exit for your guests.

    Do you offer breakout rooms?

    Absolutely. The Royalton features multiple configurations that allow for the main hall to be used in conjunction with private suites and the Sili Lounge for breakout sessions or executive meetings.
